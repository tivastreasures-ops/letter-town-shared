## What the game is (short)
Letter Town: a word-garden game for Quest 3 in the browser (WebXR hand tracking). The player sits at a table, plants and waters flowers, picks letters from flowers whose names contain them, spells words in the Spelling Book, sells things at carts, and neighbors' requests drive the story. There is a separate, visually stronger "arcade" version she likes better; Replit (Agent 4) builds a 2D edition that stays untouched until she asks.

Round 5 gave her: a 3D town (cobblestone roads, fountain, Town Hall, cottages, trees, shrubs), stone plots on the table, a fenced community garden (slow growth; the community plants too and nobody can tell who), child-size kids walking between spots, carved letter tiles popping up, her Welcome Map as the map screen, and new reach and tool-tray spacing.
