LETTER TOWN - CHAPTER ONE (Quest 3 / WebXR edition)
Built Nov 14, 2026 by the Professor (Claude) after the Director's GO.

HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE
1. Unzip. You get one folder called "lettertown".
2. Upload the whole folder to GreenGeeks inside public_html.
3. On the Quest 3, open the Quest browser and go to  https://YOUR-SITE/lettertown/   (must be https)
4. Put the controllers down so hand tracking takes over. Press "Enter VR".
It also runs on a computer or phone at the same address: click or tap everything.

HOW YOU PLAY
- The story board in front of you: poke a button with a fingertip, or point at it and pinch.
- The garden table (open the Garden tab first): pinch to pick up.
    Seeds: carry over an empty plot and let go.     Watering can: tilt the spout down over a plant.
    Super Grow: shake it over a plant.               Finished flower: pinch and pull.
  Every hand action also has a button on the Garden screen (choose a tool, then a plot).
- Settings tab: text size, volume, sound on/off, table height, reach, recenter, exit VR, erase save.
- The game saves by itself after every action. "Continue" on the title screen picks up where you left off.

WHAT IS IN THIS BUILD
- Hudson's welcome tour with all six residents, name entry, and the FLOWER / MILK practice orders.
  Practice uses its own coins, seeds and letters. Nothing in practice touches your real ones.
- Your garden: 8 plots, 4 random starters, 100 coins, 25 starting letters, 5 picks per seed, growth while away.
- Kit's shop: seeds 5/7/10, five-packs 20/30/45, Super Grow 4. Every purchase asks first.
- Spelling Book: craft real dictionary words, Word Inventory, and a Discovered list that remembers every word.
- Word Cart (6 shown + queue, oldest first, cashbox) and Flower Cart (bouquets of 3, 6 or 9, coins go straight to you).
- Noticeboard requests from neighbors.
- Mayor Ren: your garden, a choice of three cottages, a choice of three gifts, and your two-room home to decorate.
  Craft BED, CHAIR, LAMP, TABLE and other household words and place them.
- The 12-blank story "The Day the Welcome Went Missing": choose words, Surprise Me, finish, reread, retell.
- Finishing the story the first time restores the Welcome Sign (in the world and on the title screen) and
  adds Cat Girl's journal entry. After that you keep playing.
- 85 recorded voice lines play. The 20 lines without a recording show as text.

STAND-INS AND KNOWN GAPS (see the Professor's message for the full list)
- Which letters each seed grows, word payouts and sale timing are test values in rules/game.js (CFG at the top).
- The three cottages are simple drawn pictures. Cat Girl's journal words are a first draft with no voice.
- Seeds, watering can and Super Grow on the table are plain shapes.
- Nothing has been tested on a Quest yet.
